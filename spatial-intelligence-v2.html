01

The institute

Independent by architecture

Not another AI vendor.

ÉTÈ — Eternal Terra Ear — is an independent institute for computational governance. We make territory, telemetry, infrastructure and governance computationally inspectable.

From observation to action

The spatial layer connects the programme.

Remote sensing observes what happens in territory. Telecommunications reveals connectivity and infrastructure conditions. Spatial models turn those signals into geometry that can be audited.

02

ÉTÈ Kernel

Executable does not necessarily mean permitted.

The Kernel is being developed as an independent layer between an AI system's proposed physical action and runtime execution. It does not replace perception, planning or control; it evaluates action against explicit spatial, legal and operating constraints.

INPUTAI or robot proposes an action.
GEOMETRYTrajectory, boundary, proximity and spatial state.
POLICYOperating and governance constraints.
DECISIONDeterministic ALLOW / REJECT evaluation.
OUTPUTDecision before execution.

04

Validation before deployment

The Kernel is presented as a prototype and validation programme, not as a finished certified product. The independence claim has to be tested.

ALLOW

Valid trajectory

Path remains inside the permitted geometry.

REJECT

Boundary breach

Trajectory crosses a prohibited envelope.

REJECT

Proximity violation

Required separation is violated.

REJECT

Invalid state

Fault or incomplete state prevents execution.

Engineering path

Logical → edge → hardware

Simulation and isolated edge execution first; hardware-assisted enforcement only after the relevant behaviour has been independently tested.

Candidate environments

ROS2 · physics · edge systems

Validation can examine determinism, latency, fault handling and geometry compilation across controlled environments.
