ÉTÈ connects Earth observation, telecommunications, infrastructure, spatial models and governance constraints into a testable surface. The purpose is not to make AI look intelligent; it is to make physical decisions independently auditable.

01

A world model before a decision.

The workbench treats the world as coupled layers rather than a dashboard: observed conditions become signals; signals become spatial structures; structures acquire operational and governance constraints; only then can an agent's proposed action be tested.

03

Spatial intelligence becomes an independent test.

The Kernel is not the world model and not the agent. It is the boundary between a proposed physical action and what compiled constraints permit. ÉTÈ is testing this architecture; it is not presented as a certified or deployed safety system.

A · OBSERVE

Evidence field

Remote sensing, open-source spatial evidence, telecommunications state, infrastructure telemetry and relevant legal or policy sources.

B · COMPILE

Constraint surface

Spatial boundaries, safety envelopes, jurisdictional rules, topology and operating conditions become machine-checkable relationships.

C · INDEPENDENT KERNEL
ALLOW / REJECT

The agent does not decide its own admissibility. The proposed action is evaluated against an independently maintained constraint layer and produces a traceable result.

D · RECORD

Signed decision record

Decision, inputs, constraint version and evidence snapshot remain inspectable for later audit.

04

Evidence is part of the architecture.

Remote sensing naturally extends ÉTÈ's telecommunications and signal perspective: when direct access is limited, independently sourced spatial evidence can help reconstruct change, infrastructure conditions and environmental effects while retaining uncertainty.

CASE / SIGNAL · 001

Connectivity as terrain

Network measurements can be represented spatially: outage, routing fragility, interference and redundancy become properties of infrastructure rather than isolated charts.

CASE / EARTH · 002

Remote observation as evidence

Satellite imagery, SAR and change detection extend the evidence field where ground access is incomplete. The model records uncertainty instead of turning imagery into an unquestionable fact.
